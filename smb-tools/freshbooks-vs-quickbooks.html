FreshBooks vs QuickBooks: which invoicing tool fits a small business?

If you mostly need to send invoices and get paid, FreshBooks is simpler and cheaper to start. If you need real bookkeeping — bank reconciliation, reports, payroll — QuickBooks is the fuller accounting system. The crossover is about 5 clients and 3 users.

The short answer

FreshBooks Lite costs $23 a month and covers 5 billable clients, while QuickBooks Simple Start costs $38 a month for 1 user. Pick FreshBooks if invoicing is the job; pick QuickBooks if you also need bookkeeping, 3 or more users, or payroll, since QuickBooks Essentials at $85 a month includes 3 users.

Our take: Solo freelancer or shop sending under 50 invoices a month? FreshBooks Plus ($43/mo) is the sweet spot. Need multi-user bookkeeping or an accountant in the file? QuickBooks Essentials ($85/mo, 3 users) is the honest pick — and its $0 plan is a real, if limited, free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is FreshBooks cheaper than QuickBooks?
For a solo business with up to 5 billable clients, yes: FreshBooks Lite is $23 a month while QuickBooks Simple Start is $38 a month. QuickBooks becomes the better value once you need 3 or more users or full bookkeeping, because FreshBooks charges $11 a month per extra team member.
Does FreshBooks have a free plan?
No. FreshBooks offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, then a paid plan. QuickBooks Online does have a $0 plan, but it is limited to 1 user, 2 invoices a month, and 1 bank connection.
What are the card payment fees?
FreshBooks Payments charges 2.9% plus $0.30 per standard card transaction, 3.5% plus $0.30 for commercial, corporate, and Amex cards, and 1% for ACH. QuickBooks payment fees were not verified from an official page for this guide, so we do not quote them here.
