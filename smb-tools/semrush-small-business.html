Semrush for small business: worth $139.95 a month?

Semrush is the most powerful SEO toolkit a small business can buy — and the most expensive one on this hub. The honest answer: use the free account first. If 10 reports a day is not enough and you track keywords every week, Pro pays for itself. If you check rankings once a month, it does not.

Disclosure: Some links on this page may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. The comparison below uses prices verified on Semrush's own pages on 2026-11-26.

The short answer

Semrush Pro costs $139.95 a month for 5 projects and 500 tracked keywords a day, or $117.33 a month on annual billing. The free account costs $0 and includes 1 project and 10 reports a day. Start free, and pay only when you outgrow 10 reports a day.

Important — lineup rename (verified 2026-11-26): Semrush's live pricing page has been restructured to a renamed lineup — SEO, Starter, Pro+, and Advanced — replacing Pro, Guru, and Business. The prices on this page are verified on official Semrush blog and help pages, but the plan names at checkout may differ. Confirm the current plan name and price before you buy.
Our take: A local shop tracking one website and a handful of keywords should stay on the free account. A business running content marketing across 2–5 sites, checking rankings weekly, gets real value from Pro. Guru ($249.95/mo) is for agencies, not for a first SEO tool.

Checklist: free, Pro, or skip it?

  • You check rankings occasionally and run one website → Free account ($0).
  • You publish content weekly and track rankings every week → Pro ($139.95/mo).
  • You manage 6–15 client sites → Guru ($249.95/mo) — that is agency territory.
  • You only need rank tracking, not the full toolkit → the free account's 10 tracked keywords may be enough; do not pay $140 for one feature.
