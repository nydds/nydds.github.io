Calendly for small business bookings: free vs Standard vs Teams

If you take one kind of booking — say, a 30-minute consultation — Calendly's free plan does the whole job for $0. You pay when you offer several kinds of appointments, run group sessions, or want to collect payment when someone books.

The short answer

Calendly Free costs $0 forever for 1 calendar, 1 event type, and unlimited one-to-one meetings. Standard costs $10 per user a month on annual billing and adds unlimited event types, group events, and Stripe/PayPal payments. Teams costs $16 per user a month and adds round-robin scheduling.

Our take: A solo consultant, tutor, or salon with one booking type should stay free. A business with 2–3 services and a team that shares bookings should pay for Standard — $10 per user a month is the cheapest paid tier on this hub after Canva Free.

Checklist: free, Standard, or Teams?

  • One service, one person, one booking page → Free ($0).
  • Two or more services, or you take payments at booking → Standard ($10/user/mo).
  • Customers book "the next available" person across a team → Teams ($16/user/mo).
  • You only need a link that lets people pick a time → stay free; do not pay for features you will not use.

Frequently asked questions

Is Calendly free for small business?
Yes. Calendly's free plan is $0 forever and includes 1 connected calendar, 1 event type, and unlimited one-to-one meetings. You pay only when you need more event types, group events, automations, or payment collection.
How much is Calendly Standard?
Standard is $10 per user a month on annual billing. Month-to-month prices were not rendered on Calendly's official pricing page when we checked on 2026-11-26, so we quote only the verified annual-billing figure.
When should a small business pay for Calendly?
Pay when one event type is not enough — for example, you offer both consultations and paid classes — or when you need group events, automated reminders, or Stripe and PayPal payments at booking time.
