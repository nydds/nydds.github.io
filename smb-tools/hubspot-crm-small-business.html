HubSpot CRM for small business: is the free plan enough?

For most businesses under 10 people, yes — the free CRM is genuinely free, not a 14-day trap. You pay when you need more seats, more contacts, or marketing automation. The jump from free to Professional is steep, so know exactly where the free tier ends before you upgrade.

The short answer

HubSpot's free CRM costs $0 forever for 2 users, 1,000 contacts, and 2,000 email sends a month. Starter costs $20 per seat a month, and Professional starts at $890 a month with a $3,000 one-time onboarding fee. Most small businesses should stay free until they pass 2 users or 1,000 contacts.

Our take: If you have 1–2 people and under 1,000 contacts, use the free CRM and spend nothing. If you need email automation and 2,000 contacts, Starter at $20 per seat is fair. Professional at $890 a month is a growth-stage purchase, not a starting point.

Start with HubSpot's free CRM

Checklist: free, Starter, or Professional?

  • 1–2 users, under 1,000 contacts, under 2,000 emails a month → Free CRM ($0).
  • You need landing pages, more email, and a third seat → Starter ($20 per seat).
  • You need workflows, lead scoring, and real reporting → Professional (from $890/mo) — and budget the $3,000 onboarding fee.
  • You only need contacts, deals, and a shared inbox → stay free; do not buy a Hub for features you will not use.

Frequently asked questions

Is HubSpot CRM really free?
Yes. The free CRM is $0 forever for up to 2 users, 1,000 contacts, and 2,000 email sends a month. You pay only when you add paid seats or a paid Hub, starting at $20 per seat a month for Starter.
What does HubSpot Professional cost?
Marketing Hub Professional starts at $890 a month and includes 3 Core Seats, plus a one-time $3,000 onboarding fee. It is built for teams that need automation, reporting, and more than 2,000 marketing contacts.
When should a small business pay for HubSpot?
Pay when you outgrow the free limits: more than 2 users, more than 1,000 contacts, or more than 2,000 email sends a month. Until then the free CRM covers contact management, deals, and basic email.
