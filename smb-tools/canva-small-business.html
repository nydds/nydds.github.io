Canva for small business: Free vs Pro vs Business

Canva Free is enough for the occasional flyer. The moment your menu, price list, and Instagram posts need the same fonts and colors every time, Pro's Brand Kit is the reason to pay — and at $12 a person a month on annual billing, it is the cheapest Pro plan on this hub.

The short answer

Canva Pro costs $12 per person a month on annual billing, or $18 month-to-month, and includes Brand Kit, AI tools, and 100 GB of storage. Canva Free costs $0 and includes about 1.6 million templates and 5 GB of storage. Business costs $20.83 per person a month on annual billing.

Our take: One person making social posts and flyers? Start free, then pay for Pro on annual billing when you want your brand saved. A team of 3–5 making customer-facing material every week should pay for Pro — the annual plan saves 33% over month-to-month.

Checklist: free, Pro, or Business?

  • Occasional flyers and social posts, no brand rules → Free ($0).
  • You want your fonts, colors, and logo saved once and reused → Pro ($12/person/mo annual).
  • Several people design and someone must approve before publishing → Business ($20.83/person/mo annual).
  • You design once a month or less → stay free; Pro's 100 GB and AI tools only pay off with weekly use.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Canva Pro for a small business?
Canva Pro is $12 per person a month on annual billing ($144 a year) or $18 per person a month on month-to-month billing. A 30-day free trial is offered.
Is Canva Free enough for a small business?
For occasional flyers and social posts, yes: Canva Free includes about 1.6 million templates and 5 GB of storage. You need Pro when you want your brand fonts and colors saved in a Brand Kit, premium photos and video, or 100 GB of storage.
What is the difference between Canva Pro and Canva Business?
Business costs $20.83 per person a month on annual billing ($25 month-to-month) and adds up to 100 Brand Kits, 500 GB of storage, approval workflows, and real-time collaboration controls. Most small businesses do not need it; Pro is the usual pick.
