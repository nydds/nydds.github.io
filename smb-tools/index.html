The short answer

Start free where you can: HubSpot's free CRM covers 2 users and 1,000 contacts, Calendly's free plan covers one event type, and Canva Free covers basic design. Pay first for invoicing — FreshBooks Lite is $23 a month for 5 billable clients — because getting paid on time is the job that pays for the software.

Frequently asked questions

Prices were verified on each vendor's official pricing page on 2026-11-26 and are list prices, not limited-time promos. Vendors change prices, so always confirm on the official pricing page before you buy.
Do you earn money from the links on this site?

Which tool should a new small business start with?
Start with the free tier that solves your most urgent job: HubSpot's free CRM for contacts, Calendly's free plan for bookings, or Canva Free for design. Add a paid invoicing tool like FreshBooks once you send more than a few invoices a month.
